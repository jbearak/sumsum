# Episode coverage and acquisition checklist

Audited October 17, 2026, against the 700 upload records committed in `011a072`.

Where episode identities are established, this checklist counts each episode once across alternate uploads. Classic Rechov Sumsum still has provisional uploader labels and an unresolved pool of long recordings, so its candidate-upload counts are stated separately. Clips, compilations, studio-story extracts, and Word of the Day installments do not fill a gap in a core Rechov Sumsum season.

An episode link is an acquisition lead, not proof of an uncut recording. A **whole-upload candidate** has a title and length consistent with an episode, but still needs a completeness check. **Parts only** means that no single whole-program upload has been identified. **Unresolved** means the available metadata cannot establish the episode identity or the production total. No recording has been checked from beginning to end against a broadcast master.

## How to update this list

Keep the existing upload record when an alternate copy is found. Add the new URL with its provenance, then attach both record IDs to the same episode identity in the audit JSON. Mark an acquisition task complete only when a usable link has been captured. Mark a completeness task complete separately, with the exact URL, evidence, and review date. Do not use a duration threshold alone to promote a clip or story to a full program.

The detailed research notes and audit JSON linked below preserve the evidence and unresolved mappings. Production totals with conflicting or incomplete sources remain open research tasks.
