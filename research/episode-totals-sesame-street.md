# US Sesame Street episode totals

Compiled 2026-10-19 from the October 2026 sweep's three inventory agents (inv-a, seasons 1-11; inv-b, 12-24; inv-c, 25 onward). The episodes are in [guide-sesame-street.json](../data/guide-sesame-street.json), production `sesame-street`; conflicts, gaps, pilots and the announced season are in [sesame-street-episode-audit.json](../data/sesame-street-episode-audit.json). How the inventory and the links were collected is in [sesame-street-us.md](sesame-street-us.md). Nothing was watched.

## The total

**4,744 episodes aired**, from Episode 0001 on 10 November 1969 to Episode 5613 on 8 June 2026, in 56 seasons. Every episode in the guide has an air date, a quoted Muppet Wiki summary with its revision id, and a source.

The total is the sum of the Muppet Wiki season pages, each checked against the full set of individual episode pages: 1,445 for seasons 1-11, 1,690 for 12-24 and 1,609 for 25-56. inv-c also read the live English Wikipedia revision of [Sesame Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sesame_Street), which gives 4,744; a stale search-engine rendering of that article showed a different figure, and the downloaded revision was preferred. Muppet Wiki and Wikipedia are both secondary, and Wikipedia has no per-season list to check against.

Not counted:

- The five test shows of July 1969, which have no main-series number. [Test Show 1](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Sesame_Street_Test_Show_1) is dated 21 July 1969; the pages for 2-5 say only July 1969. The [Jim Henson Company Red Book](https://www.henson.com/jimsredbook/2013/07/79-181969/) confirms the July 1969 taping, not the broadcast dates.
- Specials, home video, Elmo's World as a separate series, and spin-offs. The four numbered specials, 4057, 4327, 4427 and 4552, are episodes and are counted once.
- Season 57, which is announced and has not aired (below).

The 53 episodes that Muppet Wiki's [lost-episodes page](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_episodes_of_Sesame_Street) reports missing from the Sesame Archives are counted. They aired; their loss is an archival claim, not a gap in the numbering, and no primary source confirmed it episode by episode. They are 0012 and 0105 in season 1, 23 in season 2, 25 in season 3, 0563 and 0576 in season 5, and 0996 in season 8. The audit lists them.

What the evidence column means:

- Only seasons 1, 2, 4 and 7 have a first-party count that names that season. Season 3's is a budget, and the 1981 and 1992 statements describe a typical season, not one numbered range.
- The Prime Video season labels are a retailer's catalogue, and the WarnerMedia order is a contract, not a count of aired episodes.
- Season 56's last volume (5609-5613, 8 June 2026) rests on the Muppet Wiki season and episode pages.
- For every other season the count rests on Muppet Wiki alone, confirmed by the full set of episode pages. inv-b also cites the cross-season table, which is the same wiki and not an independent source.
- The guide's season rows keep only numbers and labels. Its `totalsEvidence` cites Muppet Wiki and Wikipedia for the total; the per-season evidence above is in the inventories, not in the guide.

Sesame Workshop's [anniversary article](https://sesameworkshop.org/about-us/news/five-things-you-may-not-know-about-sesame-street/) confirms the first broadcast on 10 November 1969.

Formats, as the inventories sourced them: hour-long programmes through season 45. [PBS added a half-hour edit in 2014](https://www.pbs.org/about/about-pbs/blogs/news/pbs-kids-to-add-new-half-hour-sesame-street-program-on-air-and-on-digital-platforms-this-fall/) while keeping the hour. [Season 46](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Season_46_(2016)) moved to 30 minutes; HBO-era storefronts list about 25-26 minutes of programme. In season 56 the [producer's announcement](https://sesameworkshop.org/about-us/press-room/sesame-street-is-coming-to-netflix/) describes one 11-minute street story within the episode, and Netflix lists each episode at 30 minutes. A runtime describes a format, not the completeness of an upload.

## Numbering

Numbers run without a gap from 0001 to 4327. After that they encode the season: season 44 starts at 4401, and every later season at its season number followed by 01. The unused integers between seasons, such as 4328-4400, are not lost episodes ([Sesame Street seasons](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Sesame_Street_seasons)).

Season 45 is numbered 4501-4526 plus 4552, which makes 27 episodes. The [season 45 page](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Season_45_(2014-2015)) says 4527-4551 were assigned to repeats that kept their earlier on-screen numbers, so they are not episodes. The guide records them as `excludedNumbers` on the season. Which earlier episode each of those labels repeats is not established. The season page headlines 26 regular episodes and the cross-season table counts 27, including the special 4552; they count different things.

Numbered specials: 4057 ([The Street We Live On](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/The_Street_We_Live_On)), 4327 (listed among the specials as Sesame Street Gets Through a Storm), 4427 (Elmo's Christmas Countdown) and 4552 ([The Cookie Thief](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/The_Cookie_Thief)). 4327 and 4427 aired before the last regular episode of their seasons. Numeric order is not release order either: 4702 premiered on 7 January 2017, a week before 4701.

Seasons 41 and 42 each have 44 numbered editions: 26 new street stories and 18 reused ones. Earlier seasons reuse stories too; inv-a counted 127 repeat markers in seasons 1-11 and inv-b 261 in seasons 12-24. A reused street story does not make the numbered episode a duplicate, so each keeps its own identity.

Uploader, DVD and streaming numbering ("Season 3 Episode 12", "S26:E65", Netflix volumes) are alternate labels, never episode numbers.

## Contradictions and uncertain mappings

Each of these is in the audit's `inventoryConflicts`, `inventoryGaps` or `linkConflicts`, or in the episode's note in the guide.

- **Season 3 premiere.** The [episode 0276](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_0276) and [season 3](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Season_3_(1971-1972)) pages give 15 November 1971; the season index gives 8 November. The season page calls the Old School Volume 1 DVD menu's date wrong and cites newspaper listings, which inv-a could not open. The guide uses 15 November.
- **Season 7 premiere.** The Commissioner of Education report says the season was offered from November 1975; the episode and season pages give 1 December. The guide uses 1 December.
- **1,690 shows.** The 1981 Education Department report gives 1,690 shows for a project ending 30 September 1982, while season 13's finale is numbered 1705. The scopes differ, so it settles nothing. That it equals inv-b's 1,690 for seasons 12-24 is a coincidence.
- **2557.** [Muppet Wiki](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_2557) dates it 28 February 1989, the same date as [2562](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_2562). Every other date in the season follows the Monday-to-Friday sequence, which would put 2557 on 21 February. The guide keeps the published date and marks the episode provisional, the only provisional episode in the guide. 21 February is a calculation, not evidence.
- **2895 and 2985.** The unaired "Snuffy's Parents Get a Divorce" production is show 2895 in a research report cited by [Muppet Wiki](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Snuffy's_Parents_Get_a_Divorce) and production code 2985 in [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snuffy%27s_Parents_Get_a_Divorce). Both numbers belong to aired episodes with other stories: 2895, Oscar's brother Ernest visits (6 December 1991), and 2985, Jelani works at Birdland (10 April 1992). A [1993 interview with producer Michael Loman](https://www.csmonitor.com/1993/1122/22161.html) confirms the divorce story was withheld. The unaired production is kept apart with its number unresolved, and it does not change the season count.
- **2787.** The [8 January 1991 original](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_2787) and a [9 July rerun edition](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_2787/rerun), with another story and other sponsors, share the number. The rerun is noted under 2787 and is not an extra episode.
- **2835.** Its page redirects to [Big Bird's Birthday or Let Me Eat Cake](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Big_Bird's_Birthday_or_Let_Me_Eat_Cake), which identifies the regular-slot edition of 15 March 1991 as 2835. The prime-time pledge-drive version differs. Its sponsors are not listed.
- **0847.** Aired 10 February 1976 and was withdrawn from reruns after complaints. It is counted; it is neither unaired nor a missing number.
- **1839.** Aired 24 November 1983. Sesame Workshop's [history page](https://sesameworkshop.org/about-us/mission-and-history/) says Will Lee died in 1983; Muppet Wiki and [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr._Hooper) say 1982. That does not change the air date.
- **1092 and 1263.** 1092's infobox lists M, N and 3 as sponsors but says D, M and 2 are the ones mentioned. 1263's date is printed "March 14. 1979" and read as 14 March. Both are kept as published.
- **4715.** An [NHPBS page](https://nhpbs.org/schedule/summary.aspx?progId=SesameStreet4715) prints episode number 4715 on a Halloween story. Muppet Wiki and the producer's [Meet Julia announcement](https://sesameworkshop.org/about-us/press-room/sesame-workshop-expands-commitment-autistic-children-their-families/) identify 4715 as Meet Julia. The NHPBS title was rejected.
- **5128.** NHPBS calls it Elmo's Airlines; Muppet Wiki calls it Abby Airlines. The guide uses neither as a title.
- **5514 and 5604.** The producer's title for 5514 is "I Wonder If We Can Use This?"; Muppet Wiki has "I Wonder How We Can Use This?". The season 56 announcement called 5604 "Grover's Suity Suitcase"; Netflix released it as "Grover's Super Suitycase". The guide uses the producer's and the released titles.
- **Netflix volumes.** Netflix's seasons 1, 2 and 3 are the three volumes of production season 56: 4, 4 and 5 episodes, released 10 November 2025, 9 March 2026 and 8 June 2026. Volume 4 begins production season 57. A Sesame Workshop [press roundup](https://sesameworkshop.org/about-us/press-room/monthly-press-coverage/) quotes a news item calling the March 2026 volume the 57th season, but the producer's own volume 2 announcement keeps it in season 56.
- **HBO Max and Hulu.** HBO Max's season 42 episode 25, "Elmo Wants to Have a Ball", is 4282. Episode 26 is titled "The Biggest Loser" but described as the Furry Potter story, which is 4281. Hulu follows the same catalogue. Some of HBO Max's "season 5" snow episodes are production season 7.
- **Tubi.** Muppet Wiki's [Tubi table](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Tubi) puts Puddle Jumping at 4226; the episode pages make it 4229, and 4226 is the spider story. The link uses 4229. S19:E01 "Elmo's New Doll" is identified in the table as 2356, while its description suggests 2256. S26:E65 "The Worm Summer Games" is identified in the table as 3330, while its title suggests 3300. Those two stay possible copies and count for nothing. S31:E29 is titled "Big Bird and Snuffy Explore Things That Are Alive", but its description and the table agree on 3890, so it is linked to 3890. Tubi's [launch announcement](https://corporate.tubitv.com/press/sesame-street-arrives-on-tubi-with-250-nostalgic-episodes/) of 250 episodes through season 38 is not a current list.
- **archive.org season bundles.** Andrew Stasio's HBO and Tubi selections item leaves two labels unresolved: S05E063 "A Sesame Winter Wonderland" and S26E065 "The Worm Summer Games". The modern bundles had 49 conflicts between position labels and titles; titles decided.
- **Official YouTube chapter labels.** "Season 54 Ep. 5904" is 5409 by title, "Season 42 Ep. 44" is 4282 by title (not 4300), and "Season 42 Ep. 79", impossible in a 44-episode season, is 4279 by title.
- **Fan-made numbers.** Community uploads titled with numbers that are not episodes (4328, 4329, 4331, 4333, 4378, 4478, 4874 and others) are fan-made or mislabelled. They are recorded as unverified labels and add nothing.

## Season 57

Season 57 is announced and has not aired. [Sesame Workshop](https://sesameworkshop.org/about-us/press-room/the-sesame-street-neighborhood-is-getting-even-bigger/) and [Netflix's announcement of 24 September 2026](https://about.netflix.com/en/news/netflix-announces-sesame-street-expansion-new-shows-from-the-world-of-gabbys-dollhouse-puppy-pirates-and-more-for-preschoolers-and-kids) schedule Volume 4 for 23 November 2026, with volumes 5 and 6 planned for 2027. The [Muppet Wiki season 57 page](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Season_57_(2026-2027)) links an episode 5701 that has no page. No title, synopsis or episode count has been announced. The guide has no season 57 row; the 5701 placeholder is in the audit's `unairedOrAnnounced`. The same announcements extend the series through season 62, with no counts or dates, and nothing is inferred from that.

## Leads, not facts

- The newspaper listings behind the season 3 premiere date.
- A broadcast schedule or script that dates 2557 and numbers the divorce production.
- Which earlier episodes the season 45 repeat labels 4527-4551 repeat.
- Separate PBS dates for 5518-5533, which have only a first-run date.
- Publisher titles for 77 later episodes whose Muppet Wiki headings are not yet matched.
- First-party season counts for seasons 5, 6, 8-11, 14-23 and 25-45.
- Confirmation, episode by episode, of the 53 reported archival losses.

## Corrections to the guide

These were found while writing this note and fixed in the importer on 2026-10-19:

- The production's `years` said 1969–2025 and its total note "through season 56 (2025)". They now say 1969–2026 and that season 56 ran 2025–26, last aired 8 June 2026.
- The first `totalsEvidence` entry cited `https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Sesame_Street_Episode_Guide`, a URL that no inventory used. It now cites [Sesame Street seasons](https://muppet.fandom.com/wiki/Sesame_Street_seasons), the cross-season table inv-b cites.
- Season labels from season 12 on used a hyphen ("1980-1981"), following inv-b; all now use an en dash.

Still open: 4552 shows "The Cookie Thief" as its title while its note says the publisher title is not yet matched. inv-c took the title from Muppet Wiki's reproduction of a title card or PBS promo, a secondary transcription; 4057's title has the same basis.
