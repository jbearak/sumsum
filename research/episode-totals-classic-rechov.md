# Classic Rechov Sumsum production totals and episode audit

Audited 2026-10-17 against the existing 153 `series=classic` links in `data/sesame-records.json`. No videos were downloaded, no new video crawl was performed, and the existing catalog and UI were not edited. The complete per-upload audit, source confidence, provisional identities, and research tasks are in `data/classic-rechov-episode-audit.json`.

## Production totals

| Production | Expected total | Evidence and limit |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Original Rechov Sumsum, first broadcast in 1983 | Unresolved | Historical accounts conflict among 195 episodes across three seasons, 65 episodes for the original run, and more than 200 episodes across four or five seasons. No primary master ledger was located. Per-season totals also remain unknown. |
| Rechov Sumsum/Shara'a Simsim, Hebrew-dominant television version beginning in 1998 | 70 | An original evaluation study identifies the Hebrew version and distinguishes its broadcasts from the Arabic version. This denominator excludes home-video compilations. |
| Separate Arabic-dominant co-production broadcasts | 20 | A different broadcast version, not 20 extra Hebrew episodes. Keep a separate manifest if it enters the collection. |
| Advertised 1999 home-video tapes/compilations | No television episode denominator | Four existing links describe complete tapes, not individual broadcast episodes. A tape can reuse television footage. |

The [Revson Foundation's own project history](https://revsonfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/the_first_twenty_years_1978-1998.pdf), printed pages 8–9, establishes the original project's 1983 start but gives no original episode total. [Cole et al.'s original study](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1080/01650250344000019), production description and footnote 2, supports the separate 70 and 20 co-production versions and their 30- and 15-minute formats. Its initial 65-episode plan expanded by five. That production history does not resolve the original 1980s denominator.

The [Eli Eshed/nrg historical feature](https://www.makorrishon.co.il/nrg/online/47/ART2/523/758.html) reports 195 original episodes in three seasons during 1983–87. The [2023 Jerusalem Post feature](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/culture/article-742085) instead reports 65 for the original 1983–87 run. These are conflicting secondary claims, even though the latter includes participant interviews. The article does not say "65 per season." Multiplying it by three would be an inference. A [2006 New York Jewish Week/JTA feature](https://www.jta.org/2006/11/17/ny/open-sesame) supplies a third narrative, more than 200 episodes and four or five seasons, without a master manifest or a clear production boundary. Direct access returned 403; its indexed article text was retrieved. None of these counts is accepted as a confirmed missing-episode denominator.

## What the existing links establish

All 153 entries are community uploads, not verified broadcaster uploads. URLs are distinct. Existing classifications comprise 99 episode candidates, 50 clips, and four compilations. Of the 99 candidates, 26 carry the catalog's `uploader-described` status and 73 were inferred from runtime. Only 12 titles explicitly claim a full episode. This audit confirms no complete playback and no broadcaster master-catalog identity.

There are 11 distinct uploader-numbered identities provisionally assigned to the original production. Nine have full-upload candidates. Two are linked only through explicitly partial uploads. The nine are a count of identifiable candidates, not the total number of potentially complete episodes in the collection: another 87 original-or-unassigned long-upload candidates have no season/episode identity. The later co-production adds three unnumbered episode candidates. Thus 90 of the 99 candidate links still need a canonical crosswalk.

Current title-based production assignments are 14 original-era links with explicit season context, 17 links explicitly marked 1998/Shara, four 1999 tapes, and 118 links whose precise classic production remains unresolved. These categories cover all links, including clips. The 118 unresolved links must not automatically be credited to the original production.

## Deduplication and classification limits

Only identical URLs and explicit same-production season/episode labels were grouped. Equal runtimes, similar story titles, and upload order were not used to assert episode equivalence. Two exact advertised Hebrew story-label pairs are retained for review: Kippi Meets Gabi, `yt-LSqnlH0YZ0o` plus `yt-xQix4Pe0iZA`, and Purim, `yt-4zIQ2ohJBfg` plus `yt-LeUYIoUZz1o`. Each has a short and a full-labelled upload. Shared story or holiday wording is not proof of an identical broadcast master; neither pair reduces the unique episode count.

The 1998 [generic "episode from the series (3)" upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=73-kQzMvvXY) does not establish canonical episode 3. Its English catalog title is stronger than the Hebrew source wording. The [Full of Water upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GbjvNrgG51M) also needs a metadata review: "full" describes water in the story title, not necessarily an uncut episode. Both remain candidates, with explicit audit flags. Existing data was preserved.
