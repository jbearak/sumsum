# Shalom Sesame episode totals and linked coverage

Audited October 17, 2026 against the existing catalog at commit `011a072`. This audit changes no library or UI data and downloads no media. The full mapping, exact catalog IDs, URLs, runtimes and countable summaries are in `data/shalom-episode-audit.json`.

## What the totals mean

The classic series has **11 canonical programs**. UMass Amherst lists eleven different titles, with consecutive library call numbers CH256 through CH266. The historical Manitoba library catalog independently identifies the first five, including their official show numbers. These are program identities; several recordings of one program still count once. [UMass inventory](https://www.umass.edu/langctr/jhweb.html), [Manitoba catalog, series index on PDF page 178](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED377837.pdf#page=178).

The modern series has **12 canonical programs**. Israel Book Shop explicitly lists twelve episodes and gives their numbered titles. Shalom Sesame partner URJ independently describes twelve new episodes, and the university DVD inventory calls it a twelve-episode series. [School-series listing](https://www.israelbookshop.com/products/shalom-sesame-series-for-schools-set-of-12-dvd), [URJ partnership announcement](https://urj.org/press-room/reformjudaismorg-partners-shalom-sesame-offer-engaging-jewish-content-kids), [university inventory, page 13](https://oru.edu/pdfs/current-students/my-academics/resources/hebrew_culture_dvds.pdf#page=13).

All eleven classic identities have candidate coverage. Nine have a candidate named for the canonical program. The other two, The People of Israel and Jerusalem, rely on generic uploader titles that state episode 4 and episode 5. The primary Manitoba series index explicitly maps Show 4 to The People of Israel and Show 5 to Jerusalem. This mapping does not depend on the order of uploads. Actual title cards and start-to-credits completeness still need review. [Primary numbering index](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED377837.pdf#page=178).

The catalog currently has 53 classic Shalom uploads and 257 modern Shalom uploads. Neither figure is an episode total. Songs, previews, short scenes, duplicate recordings, openings and numbered parts account for most upload records.

## Evidence limits

"Title-verified candidate" means that existing metadata supports the episode identity and the upload has a plausible program-length runtime. It does not certify uninterrupted playback, an uncut master, current availability or rights. No entry in this audit is promoted to "complete-upload linked." The JSON includes every associated catalog ID and URL, distinguishes episode parts from studio-story parts and trailers, and lists the unassigned clips that were excluded from episode counts.

Two source inconsistencies are recorded explicitly. The media guide misprints Kids Sing Israel as number 1 although its other titles cover 1–10. The retailer lists Shows 1–11 in detail while its introductory copy says twelve programs on six discs. The eleven-program classic total follows the enumerated library and numbered program lists, rather than that inconsistent retailer headline.
