# Modern Rechov Sumsum, short series and Israeli Elmo: totals and coverage

Updated 2026-10-18. The first version of this audit (2026-10-17) found no episode list for any modern season. Archived Hop! pages, library catalogues and the regulator's reports now give totals for three of the four core seasons and titles for part of each. The machine-readable audit is [hop-episode-audit.json](../data/hop-episode-audit.json), version 2. Episode identities and coverage are in [episode-guide.json](../data/episode-guide.json). The audit's research loaded no Hop! HTML page by script. Afterwards, on 18 October, the Hop collector read all 234 catalogued Hop! pages in Zen Home through the Hop-only Proton rule to collect their Vimeo links (see "New Hop! posts and removed items"). No VPN setting changed, and no media was downloaded.

## 2006 Hop! revival

Hop!'s own archived [2007 series page](https://web.archive.org/web/20071013054127/http://hop.co.il:80/sesameNew/html/aboutInfo.html) says "הסדרה כוללת 40 פרקים". [TheMarker](https://www.themarker.com/advertising/2006-10-09/ty-article/0000017f-e572-df5f-a17f-fffec69e0000) reported 40 episodes of 15 minutes in October 2006. That moves the 40 from secondary press reports to a producer statement.

Six titles come from two DVDs, each holding three complete episodes. Classicaletet issued "רחוב סומסום-המיטב של מוישה אופניק" and "רחוב סומסום-המיטב של אביגיל" in January 2007 (NLI records), and Sisu issued them with English subtitles as [Meet Oofnik the Grouch](https://web.archive.org/web/20120126094229/http://www.sisuent.com:80/get_item_v1311+dv_meet-oofnik-the-grouch-dvd.htm) and [Avigile & Friends](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005224034/http://www.sisuent.com:80/i/3964/avigile-friends-israeli-children-s-dvd.htm). The titles are What to Do When You're Angry, Karambula and The Dwarf and the Giant, then Avigile's Paintings, Avigile's Friends and Avigile Is Sad.

Two Hop! labels give numbers without titles. The Rabbi Binyamin Lau guest segment (hop-5115) is labelled "רחוב סומסום 2006 -פרק 15". Hop! music post hop-9867 is the same Vimeo video and is linked the same way. An archived Hop! API capture ties the documentary about Uri, a blind girl, to the code SUM_2006_e16; that item is gone from Hop!, and its official YouTube copy yt-PpoUJpucP_Q is linked as an excerpt. These two rows may be the same programmes as titled ones, so the season has six to eight identified episodes.

Karambula is the only whole candidate. Sesame Workshop's licensed package "Sesame Street From Around the World" on Prime Video includes it as its Israel episode; "S1 E10" there is the package's numbering. An archive.org copy with English subtitles calls it "פרק 30" in its description, which is a community label, not a broadcaster number. An IMDb clip of 13:01 described as a sneak preview of the Avigile & Friends DVD (web-6b6a7cd601) waits for identification. Between 32 and 34 of the 40 titles are unknown.

## 2009 season

A Hop! statement republished by [TVNetil](https://www.tvnetil.net/report/3268/) gives 28 episodes, and the regulator's 12 and 16 sum to the same. A [July 2009 ynet report](https://www.ynet.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-3741464,00.html) planned 30.

Hop!'s archived [2010 educators' page](https://web.archive.org/web/20100301223744/http://www.hop.co.il:80/sesameNew/html/aboutTeachers2010.htm) names three episodes: "הצבע לא קובע", "המסעדה של כרובי" and "שיר המקהלה". Hop! labels tie segments to episodes 1 (בנאים, hop-5112), 2 (הדג נחש, hop-5113) and 4 (Achinoam Nini and Mira Awad, hop-5114). Hop! music posts hop-9860, hop-9857 and hop-9865 are the same Vimeo videos and are linked the same way. An old-site code, SUM_2009_e6, names a Keren Leibovitch guest segment of episode 6 that is no longer on hop.co.il.

Hop!'s 17 current 2009 story files run 3:12 to 6:11, against 16.51 net minutes accounted per episode. They are segments of unidentified episodes, and the audit now classes them that way. Hop!'s web numbers 1-17 follow the old site's SUM_m1-m17 order, not broadcast order. Two files resemble documented titles ("המקהלה", "כל צבע הוא טוב") without matching them. Two uploads on actress Shosha Goren's channel, labelled "שלום סומסום 2" and "3", show Hop! 2009 sets and logo in their preview frames, so they also wait in the 2009 queue. Between 21 and 24 of the 28 titles are unknown.

## Discovering Curiosity, 2015

The total is contested. Hop!'s premiere text for 15 March 2015, reproduced on a [blog](http://revital-world.blogspot.com/2015/03/2015-15-2015-82-77.html), says "כוללת 29 פרקים". The 2014 report lists a separately named unit, "סומסום - 2014 מגלים סקרנות", of 4 episodes from 26 December 2014. Whether those 4 belong to the 29 is unresolved.

Hop!'s [category 321](https://hop.co.il/wp-json/wp/v2/shows?shows-category=321&per_page=100), checked on 18 October 2026, has 27 items: 10 narratives, 2 experiment films, 13 science-word shorts and 2 riddles. An eleventh narrative, "לחמם את האף" (hop-5096), is filed in the parent רחוב סומסום category. The first version of the audit counted it as a segment and the two experiment films as narratives. Version 2 reverses both.

Each story now cites its own archived 2015 Hop! page. Nine pages describe the story as "פרקים מלאים לילדים של רחוב סומסום - מגלים סקרנות". The pages for "התיק של אלמו" (hop-5093) and "לחמם את האף" (hop-5096) say only "פרקים של רחוב סומסום- מגלים סקרנות", so for those two the whole-candidate role rests on the listing and on a length within the range of the nine labelled stories. For hop-5096 there is more support. Its official YouTube copy yt-zLgFM0fqFEI is titled "רחוב סומסום: לחמם את האף - מגלים סקרנות בהופ!", and a Tel Aviv University copy names Prof. Liad Mudrik as a guest.

The narrative files run 5:21 to 8:52. Six are shorter than the 7.1 net minutes per episode accounted in 2014: hop-5086 (5:21), hop-5096 (5:26), hop-5085 (6:12), hop-5091 (6:17), hop-5083 (6:46) and hop-5075 (7:03). The 7.1 figure belongs to a separately named 4-episode unit whose link to these stories is unresolved, so the runtime neither establishes nor rules out a whole programme. For the five of those six whose pages say "פרקים מלאים", the whole-candidate role rests on that label; hop-5096 is the weakest whole candidate of the season. Each guide note gives its own file's length.

A [2016 Ministry of Education guide](https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/PreSchool/Word-of-the-day.pdf) names one more episode, "מה קרה לקרטיבים?", which has no upload. It also mentions an episode about the moon's phases, probably "הירח". An off-screen phone recording labelled "פרק 1" (yt-rcjMhVLd1IE) has no story title and waits in the queue. It and three official YouTube copies whose only full-episode wording is the series description (yt-yojxEBKFtJQ, yt-8_N9CRpJank and yt-7D71gr9NUXI) are catalogued as unconfirmed episodes, like the Hop! files of the same stories.

## Sesame Stories, 2003

Hop!'s archived [teachers' booklet](https://web.archive.org/web/20040118125430/http://www.hop.co.il:80/sumsum/ganim/prospect.asp) says "בסדרה 26 פרקים", and Sesame Workshop's [2004 Israel page](https://web.archive.org/web/20040620054435/http://www.sesameworkshop.org:80/international/il/eng/home.php) says the same. Hop!'s archived [2004 schedule](https://web.archive.org/web/20040812113108/http://www.hop.co.il:80/sumsum/ganim/sched.asp) lists exactly 26 distinct titles with goals and synopses, but no episode numbers. The series probably premiered on Sunday 31 August 2003; a Maariv preview of 26 August, republished on Hop!'s press page, says "ביום ראשון, עם החזרה ללימודים". English titles in the guide are editorial.

One title has a whole candidate. Sesame Workshop International's yt-hmCV2gvg_s4 (24:11, Hebrew with English subtitles) is titled Rechov Sumsum, but its tags and description match "נוח וברוש לא חברים". That identification is provisional. The other 25 titles have no link. Warshel's dissertation says 21 of the 26 were dubbed into Arabic. Hop!'s current "סיפורי סומסום" group (hop-4722 "מיחזור", hop-5107 "נקיון חופים" and hop-5108 "אלמוגים", 2:04 to 2:34) is not mapped to this series, but that is unresolved. Nothing dates the clips: their February 2024 post dates are Hop!'s site migration, and their only label is the group name. Classicaletet's 2007 DVD of songs and stories from the series ([NLI record 990028077290205171](https://nli.alma.exlibrisgroup.com/view/sru/972NNL_INST?version=1.2&operation=searchRetrieve&query=alma.mms_id=990028077290205171)) lists songs titled "מיחזור" and "נקיון חופים", the titles of two of the clips, and "שיר ההתחשבות" (hop-5165; music post hop-9876 is the same Vimeo video). The catalogue already files a "מיחזור" song from that DVD (yt-Jk6wByOMm5o) under this series. Comparing hop-4722 and hop-5107 with the DVD songs would settle it. An Elmo tag on hop-5108 came from the letters of its title, אלמוגים (corals), and was removed. Classicaletet's 2007 DVD "רחוב סומסום-שירים וסיפורים" holds songs and stories from the series, not whole episodes.

## New Hop! posts and removed items

Hop!'s public WordPress API holds 49 Rechov Sumsum posts that were outside the original 185-post manifest. They were added to [hop-records.json](../data/hop-records.json) and appended to [hop-url-manifest.json](../data/hop-url-manifest.json) and the collector's embedded manifest:

- 6 resilience posts in "המרחב המוגן של הופ!", with no show category (hop-4927 to hop-4943);
- 42 posts of the separate "music" post type in music category 253 "רחוב סומסום", at /music/ paths;
- 1 Tu BiShvat special, "מעבדה – אביגיל שואלת איך ולמה" (hop-10133), which may be Discovering Curiosity material.

On 18 October an agent collected their Vimeo links in Zen Home through the Hop-only Proton rule, at the user's request. All 234 manifest pages returned a Vimeo player link, and each new link keeps its privacy hash; [hop.md](hop.md) describes the run. The 49 posts run 0:53 to 26:17 by Vimeo's metadata. hop-10133 runs 6:52 and stays unverified, because its running time alone does not make it a whole programme.

The 234 pages hold 224 distinct Vimeo videos. Ten music posts embed the same video as a catalogued show post, so the 49 posts add 39 videos. Four of the ten share videos with Hop!-labelled segments. hop-9860, hop-9857 and hop-9865 are the same videos as hop-5112, hop-5113 and hop-5114, from 2009 episodes 1, 2 and 4, and hop-9867 is the same video as hop-5115, from 2006 episode 15. The four are linked to the same episodes as excerpts, and those episodes stay excerpt-only. hop-9874 "הצבע לא קובע" is the same video as the song hop-5168; neither is linked to the 2009 episode of that title. The other five repeats are four songs and the fifty-year song compilation (hop-9862, the same video as hop-5110). Three same-title pairs are different videos: hop-6848 and hop-5116, hop-6963 and hop-5117, and hop-6960 and hop-6961. This comparison replaces the earlier estimate that about 15 music titles duplicate show posts: 12 music posts share a show post's title or nearly so, and 10 of those are the same video.

No link was played. The audit lists the posts, their links and the comparison under `newHopPosts` in [hop-episode-audit.json](../data/hop-episode-audit.json). [TODO.md](../TODO.md) keeps the collection steps for pages added later.

Some items are no longer on hop.co.il and cannot be collected: SUM_2009_e6 (the Keren Leibovitch segment), "אלמו לומד לנצח בכבוד", SUM_2006_e16 (the Uri documentary, which survives on YouTube), SUM_speical_e54 and SUM_m27.
