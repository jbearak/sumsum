# Modern Rechov Sumsum program formats

Checked October 17, 2026. The library now separates complete installments of short series from full episodes of the core program.

## What the primary sources establish

The [Council for Cable TV and Satellite Broadcasting's 2006 report, printed page 141](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/moatsa2006/he/%D7%93%D7%95%D7%97%202006%20%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%A6%D7%94.pdf) lists the new Rechov Sumsum season on Hop, a first transmission date of December 16, 2006, and an episode length of 15 minutes. The ten episodes in that annual accounting are not necessarily the complete commissioned season. A universal minimum of twenty minutes is therefore unsupported for the modern reboot.

The [Council's 2010 report, printed page 67](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/moatsa2010/he/%D7%93%D7%95%D7%97%202010%20%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%A6%D7%94.pdf) lists a new Rechov Sumsum season, sixteen episodes, a first transmission date of January 5, 2010, and an episode-length figure of 16.51 under net minutes. Its footnote states that the production receives recognition for 64 percent of initial transmission time because it combines material that is not locally produced. This is a regulatory production accounting table. The figure should not be inflated to twenty-five minutes by dividing it by 64 percent, nor should the accounting total be treated as a list of complete downloadable episodes.

The [Council's 2013 report, printed page 139](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/moatsa2013/he/%D7%93%D7%95%D7%97%202013%20%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%A6%D7%94_0.pdf) lists Rechov Sumsum 2012 as a new original Israeli season, fifteen episodes, nine net minutes per episode, and first transmission on January 13, 2013. This establishes a shorter format in official accounting. It does not establish that each current Hop web asset preserves an entire broadcast program.

These government PDFs returned access errors when opened directly through the web reader, but the search service exposes the indexed tables and the 2010 footnote. The findings above use that indexed primary document text. The source URLs identify the actual government reports, not a secondary account.

## What the current video files establish

The [official Hop collection](https://hop.co.il/shows/%D7%A8%D7%97%D7%95%D7%91-%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%9D/) groups seventeen web entries under Rechov Sumsum 2009 and fifteen under Rechov Sumsum 2012. It labels entries with episode numbers. That wording alone does not distinguish a narrative segment from the complete magazine program that incorporated it.

All seventeen 2009 assets have public Vimeo metadata supplied by Hoptv. Their lengths range from 3:12 to 6:11. For example, [Ofnik's kite](https://vimeo.com/908692932/6816b6cb85) runs 5:52, as verified by its [Vimeo metadata](https://vimeo.com/api/oembed.json?url=https%3A%2F%2Fvimeo.com%2F908692932%2F6816b6cb85). These files are much shorter than the modern season length reported around that period. Treating them as complete broadcast magazine episodes would require additional evidence.

The fifteen 2012 assets range from 1:35 to 13:18. [Everyone has something special](https://vimeo.com/908689000/9472db2231) runs 1:35. [Ofnik moves house](https://vimeo.com/908688663/61aba3f12e) runs 12:08. [The princess, the dragon and the tree](https://vimeo.com/908689489/c8bcc73a25) runs 13:18. The lengths are recorded in the corresponding public Vimeo oEmbed responses and in `data/vimeo-metadata.json`. The mix of lengths prevents a single length threshold from establishing completeness. No explicit proof was found that these current assets are complete original magazine programs.

Word of the Day 2023 is a separate short format series. Its eight Hop files run 5:41 to 6:27. The corresponding official YouTube titles explicitly call them full episodes of that series. For instance, [Hug on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jricljQkc3o) is labelled as a full Word of the Day episode, while [the Hop/Hoptv Vimeo version](https://vimeo.com/916707529/6cdb440a7c) runs 6:14. That supports calling it a complete short format installment. It does not make it a full episode of the core Rechov Sumsum magazine series.

The older Word of the Day clips form another group, with twenty-five files running 1:16 to 2:06. The site also mixes songs, guest appearances, short stories, dubbed American Sesame material, and the separate Israeli Elmo talk show into its wider Rechov Sumsum collection. A shared collection name does not establish a shared program format.

## Conclusion for classification

The user's distinction between the core show and six minute Word of the Day installments is justified. The catalog should not present these as equivalent complete programs. However, primary records show that some modern formats were shorter than twenty minutes. Complete core episodes need evidence of program identity and completeness; duration can assist screening but is not sufficient proof. The thirty-two current assets filed under 2009 and 2012 should retain unverified complete program status until such evidence is found.
