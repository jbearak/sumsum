# Modern Rechov Sumsum program formats

Checked October 17, 2026; regulator citations and the 2009 and 2012 conclusions updated October 18, 2026. The library separates complete installments of short series from full episodes of the core program. Season totals and episode lists are in [episode-totals-hop.md](episode-totals-hop.md).

## What the primary sources establish

The [Council for Cable TV and Satellite Broadcasting's 2006 report](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/moatsa2006/he/%D7%93%D7%95%D7%97%202006%20%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%A6%D7%94.pdf) lists the new Rechov Sumsum season on Hop with an episode length of 15 minutes. The HOT appendix, printed page 130, counts 18 episodes from December 15, 2006. The yes appendix, printed page 141, counts 10 from December 16. Hop's own 2007 page gives the season as 40 episodes, so neither annual count is the season total. A universal minimum of twenty minutes is therefore unsupported for the modern reboot.

The [Council's 2010 report, printed page 139](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/moatsa2010/he/%D7%93%D7%95%D7%97%202010%20%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%A6%D7%94.pdf) lists a new Rechov Sumsum season, sixteen episodes, a first transmission date of January 5, 2010, and an episode-length figure of 16.51 under net minutes. Its footnote states that the production receives recognition for 64 percent of initial transmission time because it combines material that is not locally produced. This is a regulatory production accounting table. The figure should not be inflated to twenty-five minutes by dividing it by 64 percent, nor should the accounting total be treated as a list of complete downloadable episodes. The [2009 report, printed page 130](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/moatsa2009/he/%D7%93%D7%95%D7%97%202009%20%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%A6%D7%94.pdf) counts the first 12 episodes of the same season from December 13, 2009, with the same 16.51 figure. An earlier version of this note cited printed page 67 of the 2010 report; that page is about EPRA.

The [Council's 2013 report, printed page 139](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/moatsa2013/he/%D7%93%D7%95%D7%97%202013%20%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%A6%D7%94_0.pdf) lists Rechov Sumsum 2012 as a new original Israeli season, fifteen episodes, nine net minutes per episode, and first transmission on January 13, 2013. This establishes a shorter format in official accounting. It does not establish that each current Hop web asset preserves an entire broadcast program.

The [2014 report, printed pages 146-147](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/moatsa2014/he/%D7%93%D7%95%D7%97%202014.pdf) lists "סומסום - 2014 מגלים סקרנות", four episodes of 7.1 net minutes from December 26, 2014. From 2015 the Council stopped publishing per-production tables.

The first check read these reports through indexed text because the web reader returned access errors. On October 18 the PDFs were downloaded and every Hop table in the HOT and yes appendices was read.

## What the current video files establish

The [official Hop collection](https://hop.co.il/shows/%D7%A8%D7%97%D7%95%D7%91-%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%9D/) groups seventeen web entries under Rechov Sumsum 2009 and fifteen under Rechov Sumsum 2012. It labels entries with episode numbers. That wording alone does not distinguish a narrative segment from the complete magazine program that incorporated it.

All seventeen 2009 assets have public Vimeo metadata supplied by Hoptv. Their lengths range from 3:12 to 6:11. For example, [Oofnik's kite](https://vimeo.com/908692932/6816b6cb85) runs 5:52, as verified by its [Vimeo metadata](https://vimeo.com/api/oembed.json?url=https%3A%2F%2Fvimeo.com%2F908692932%2F6816b6cb85). These files are much shorter than the 16.51 net minutes accounted per episode. They are segments of broadcast episodes that are not yet identified, and Hop's web numbers 1-17 follow its old site order, not broadcast order.

The fifteen 2012 assets range from 1:35 to 13:18. [Everyone has something special](https://vimeo.com/908689000/9472db2231) runs 1:35. [Oofnik moves house](https://vimeo.com/908688663/61aba3f12e) runs 12:08. [The princess, the dragon and the tree](https://vimeo.com/908689489/c8bcc73a25) runs 13:18. The lengths are recorded in the corresponding public Vimeo oEmbed responses and in `data/vimeo-metadata.json`. The mix of lengths prevents a single length threshold from establishing completeness. Twelve of the fifteen are now matched to the season's 15 episodes through Hop's DVD set and its archived 2015 pages, which call them full episodes. Those files run 9:53 to 13:18, more than the nine net minutes accounted per episode. They are whole-programme candidates, not verified complete programmes. The 1:35 file matches no documented episode title and is far shorter than an episode.

Word of the Day 2023 is a separate short format series. Its eight Hop files run 5:41 to 6:27. The corresponding official YouTube titles explicitly call them full episodes of that series. For instance, [Hug on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jricljQkc3o) is labelled as a full Word of the Day episode, while [the Hop/Hoptv Vimeo version](https://vimeo.com/916707529/6cdb440a7c) runs 6:14. That supports calling it a complete short format installment. It does not make it a full episode of the core Rechov Sumsum magazine series.

The older Word of the Day clips form another group, with twenty-five files running 1:16 to 2:06. A Ministry of Education guide shows that each is the opening segment of a 10-15 minute "מילת היום ברחוב סומסום" programme made as part of the 2012 season. The site also mixes songs, guest appearances, short stories, dubbed American Sesame material, and the separate Israeli Elmo talk show into its wider Rechov Sumsum collection. A shared collection name does not establish a shared program format.

## Conclusion for classification

The user's distinction between the core show and six minute Word of the Day installments is justified. The catalog should not present these as equivalent complete programs. However, primary records show that some modern formats were shorter than twenty minutes. Complete core episodes need evidence of program identity and completeness; duration can assist screening but is not sufficient proof. On that basis the 2009 story files are segments awaiting identification, and the twelve matched 2012 files are whole-programme candidates whose completeness is still unverified.
