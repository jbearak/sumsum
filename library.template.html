About this library

This is an index of public video pages and collections found during research. It does not contain video files. Counts refer to upload links and source pages; the same material can appear more than once. Some sources list a whole collection instead of individual clips; those entries are marked Collection. The Full episodes filter uses uploader or publisher descriptions; completeness has not been checked by watching each upload in full. Long videos classified only by runtime, and uploads whose completeness is uncertain, appear under Unconfirmed episodes. Explicit parts and excerpts remain Clips.

Short-form episodes are listings of complete installments in separate short series, such as Word of the day 2023. They do not establish that a complete episode of the core Rechov Sumsum series is available. No complete modern core broadcast episode has been verified in this index.

Hop may require an Israeli connection to open a video page. When a Vimeo link is available, the watch button opens it directly. The video host may still restrict playback by country. Availability can change. Opening a link does not change your VPN connection.

To add an unlisted Vimeo link, open a Hop item, follow its Vimeo link, and copy the complete address. Open the item's Details and save it here. Keep the full link, including its privacy hash. Your links and saved videos stay in this browser. Export a backup before moving this file or changing browsers.

Search and filters work offline. Watching videos requires an internet connection. No tracking, external fonts, or third-party scripts.
